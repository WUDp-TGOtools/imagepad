**Summary (max 132 characters):**
Prepare images for upload beside any page: crop, blur, watermark, then drag into the site. Nothing is uploaded.

**Description:**
ImagePad is a small helper for the moment you need to change a picture before you upload it, but opening a big editor is overkill.

It opens beside the page you are on, in the browser's side panel. You can:
- Crop to a free area, or to common site frames such as profile photos, Instagram posts and stories, video thumbnails, and banners.
- Blur, pixelate or cover parts of a picture with a rectangle, an ellipse or a brush.
- Add a text watermark in a corner or tiled over the whole image.
- Set the format (JPEG, WebP or PNG), quality, a maximum file size and pixel size.
- Drag the finished image straight into the page, copy it, or save it.

Everything runs on your device. ImagePad does not upload images, has no analytics and no account. Metadata (camera, date and GPS location) is removed by default; you can keep it for JPEG files if you need it.

A guided tour runs on first start and can be replayed from the help button.

ImagePad is part of WUDp by TGO.tools.

**Permissions and why:**
- Side panel: shows the editor next to the page.
- Context menu: "Edit with ImagePad" on images.
- Storage: saves your tool and output settings on this device.
